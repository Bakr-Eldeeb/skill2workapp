## Meaning and boundaries

“Assessed” means scored, not accredited, passed or genuinely AI-graded. Practice results disclose the local scoring rules in assessment/results/details and Technology. Authentication/state/tickets/media are local to this browser origin; storage events do not synchronize different devices. QR registration tickets are presentation artifacts, not secure admission credentials. Future server contracts and privacy work are described in API_INTEGRATION.md.
